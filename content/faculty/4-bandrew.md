Benjamin Andrew teaches at Pennsylvania State University, focusing on online education and interdisciplinary courses. He has worked as a graphic and web designer for clients ranging from political campaigns and activist groups to international research teams at NASA. His visual art practice explores the frontiers of storytelling by leveraging participatory art and new media to imagine strange new futures. He previously taught at the Maryland Institute College of Art and Johns Hopkins University. He misses Baltimore but is enjoying his new home on the Internet.
