Courtney "Cookie" Redding is an adjunct lecturer with the School of Visual Arts and teaches courses in the DMD Program. Her work encompasses the art and design world, with a focus on a multiple media forms of expression. Redding's influences are from a diverse array of disciplines. From science and history to the natural world and technological field, her imagery integrates these backgrounds into a philosophy of symbols and a personal typographic and textural array.
