Jacob Weinberg is an artist, creative technician, and educator living and working in Philadelphia, PA. Working with video, sound, electronics, and installation, Jacob operates intimately within technical dimensions of mechanisms that haunt and shape contemporary conditions of rationality, uncertainty, and subjectivity. Alongside his studio practice, Jacob works with artists and companies to design and produce dynamic multimedia systems and content for performance, immersive installation, and film. His work has recently been broadcast in radio festivals in London and Glasgow and he has recently worked on production for projects appearing at the Okayama Art Summit (Okayama Japan) and the Icebox Project space in Philadelphia, PA.
