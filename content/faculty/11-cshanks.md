Christine J. Shanks teaches and creates work in both traditional studio and digital media. She applies her design background to her multifaceted work in academia by utilizing user-experience principles, creativity, and design thinking in her curriculum and pedagogy. As an educator her primary goal is to establish a supportive and inclusive classroom that emboldens students to explore and refine their process, motivations, and skills. She feels her work in academia and design are intertwined, new projects and pursuits in one field develop from ideas and exploration in the other.
