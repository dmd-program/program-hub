Anna Divinsky is an Associate Teaching Professor of Integrative Arts at the College of Arts and Architecture with two decades of teaching experience. She is also the Digital Multimedia Design and Digital Arts Certificate Program Coordinator. Her research focuses on exploring the best teaching and learning practices through the innovative use of technology. Her passion for handmade art and personalized teaching inform her approach to online education. In addition to teaching undergraduates, Divinsky offers professional development and community building opportunities to the graduate students at the College of Arts and Architecture.

Divinsky is a fiber artist with a background in installation and printmaking. Her work explores repetitive mark making and organic forms through meditative processes. 

Originally from Kyiv, Ukraine, Divinsky resides (and teaches remotely) in Pittsburgh, PA with her husband, two sons, and a three-legged dog named Panda.
