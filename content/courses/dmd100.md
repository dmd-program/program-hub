DMD 100 builds design process and thinking skills so you can navigate ambiguous, ill-defined problem spaces with confidence.

## Course Overview

We introduce the shared language, tools, and mindsets of art and design, communications, and information sciences. You will practice moving from research and framing through iteration, critique, and delivery—using design as a lens to uncover and shape solutions.

## You'll Learn to

- Apply design thinking to messy, real-world challenges
- Move from discovery to definition to delivery using repeatable methods
- Collaborate and critique effectively across disciplines
- Communicate concepts with clear visuals and narratives
- Reflect on process to improve future work

## Studio Rhythm

- Weekly: short design sprints with critique and iteration
- Midterm: concept development with stakeholder feedback
- Final: a polished artifact plus process documentation

## Core Ideas to Carry Forward

- Design is a frame for inquiry, not just output.
- Process beats perfection—iterate, test, and refine.
- Clear storytelling unlocks adoption of your ideas.
