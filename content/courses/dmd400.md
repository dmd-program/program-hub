DMD 400: Digital Multimedia Design Capstone is the culminating course where students apply everything they've learned to create a comprehensive portfolio project.

## Course Overview

This capstone course allows students to demonstrate their mastery of digital multimedia design through the development of a substantial portfolio project. Students apply creative production concepts, tools, and approaches to address contemporary issues while showcasing their unique skills and vision.

## What You'll Create

- A comprehensive capstone portfolio project
- Professional documentation of your process
- A presentation showcasing your work
- Reflection on your learning journey

## Requirements

Students are expected to work independently while receiving guidance and feedback from instructors and peers. The project should demonstrate technical proficiency, creative vision, and critical thinking skills developed throughout the program.
