The Generalist pathway is designed for students who want the flexibility to explore multiple areas of digital multimedia design without specializing in a single track.

## Overview

This pathway allows you to:
- Sample courses from all three colleges
- Build a diverse skill set
- Create a personalized learning experience
- Prepare for multifaceted career opportunities

## Recommended Courses

Students in the Generalist pathway should take a balanced selection of courses from:
- Arts & Architecture
- Communications
- Information Sciences & Technology

## Career Opportunities

Graduates from this pathway are prepared for roles such as:
- Creative Director
- Design Strategist
- Digital Producer
- Multimedia Specialist
