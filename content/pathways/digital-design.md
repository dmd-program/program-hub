The Digital Design pathway prepares students to become creative professionals who can design compelling visual experiences across digital platforms.

## Overview

This pathway combines artistic vision with technical skill, emphasizing:
- Visual design principles and theory
- User experience (UX) and user interface (UI) design
- Digital illustration and graphics
- Design thinking and problem-solving

## Core Competencies

### Visual Design
- Typography and layout
- Color theory and application
- Composition and hierarchy
- Brand identity and systems

### Digital Tools
- Adobe Creative Suite mastery
- Prototyping and wireframing tools
- Design collaboration platforms
- Version control for designers

### User Experience
- User research and testing
- Information architecture
- Interaction design
- Accessibility and inclusive design

## Career Paths

Digital Design graduates work as:
- UX/UI Designer
- Graphic Designer
- Visual Designer
- Brand Designer
- Product Designer
- Motion Graphics Designer
- Art Director

## Recommended Courses

Key courses for this pathway include:
- Introduction to Visual Studies (ART 010)
- Introduction to Drawing (ART 020)
- 2D Digital Arts (DART 202)
- The Art of Web Design (DART 206)
- Foundations of Human-Centered Design (IST 331)

## Portfolio Development

Students build a professional portfolio featuring:
- Brand identity systems
- Website and app designs
- Digital illustrations
- User experience case studies
- Motion graphics projects
