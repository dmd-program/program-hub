# Media for Civic Engagement

The Media for Civic Engagement pathway empowers students to use media, design, and storytelling to address social issues, amplify community voices, and create positive change.

## Core Philosophy

This pathway combines media production skills with critical thinking about social justice, community engagement, and the role of media in democratic society.

## Key Focus Areas

- Documentary and advocacy media
- Community-based storytelling
- Social media for social change
- Participatory design and co-creation
- Media literacy and critical analysis
- Ethical considerations in representation

## Essential Courses

### Media Production
- **COMM 180** - Summary of Electronic Media and Telecommunications
- **COMM 282** - Web Design and Development
- **COMM 342** - Idea Development and Media Writing
- **COMM 481** - Documentary Production

### Critical Studies
- **COMM 242** - Media, Diversity, and Social Change
- **CAS 271** - Rhetorical Theory and Criticism
- **WMNST 105** - Introduction to Women's Studies
- **ANTH 045** - Introduction to Cultural Anthropology

### Design & Communication
- **DART 200** - Digital Multimedia Design Foundations
- **COMM 215** - Effective Communication
- **COMM 310** - Intercultural Communication

## Career Paths

Graduates pursue careers in:
- Nonprofit media and communications
- Community organizing and advocacy
- Documentary filmmaking
- Social media strategy for NGOs
- Public interest design
- Educational media production
- Participatory action research

## Project Examples

Students create impactful work such as:
- Documentary films about local communities
- Social media campaigns for causes
- Interactive storytelling platforms
- Community mapping projects
- Public service announcements

Opportunities to work with:

- Educational institutions
- Government agencies
- International development projects
