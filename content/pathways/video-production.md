The Video Production pathway prepares students to become skilled video producers and storytellers, capable of creating compelling content for film, television, web, and social media platforms.

## Overview

This pathway emphasizes:
- Visual storytelling and narrative development
- Professional video production techniques
- Post-production and editing workflows
- Distribution strategies for multiple platforms

## Core Skills Developed

### Pre-Production
- Scriptwriting and storyboarding
- Project planning and budgeting
- Location scouting and casting

### Production
- Camera operation and cinematography
- Lighting design and techniques
- Audio recording and management
- Directing and crew management

### Post-Production
- Video editing and color grading
- Motion graphics and visual effects
- Sound design and mixing
- Final delivery and distribution

## Career Opportunities

Graduates from the Video Production pathway pursue careers as:
- Video Producer
- Cinematographer
- Video Editor
- Content Creator
- Documentary Filmmaker
- Broadcast Producer
- Social Media Video Specialist

## Recommended Courses

Students in this pathway should prioritize courses such as:
- Video Production (COMM 282)
- Idea Development and Media Writing (COMM 342)
- Photography for the Mass Media (COMM 469)
- Digital Arts courses focusing on motion graphics

Our Video Production students benefit from:
- Real-world project opportunities
